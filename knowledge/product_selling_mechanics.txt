# Product Selling Mechanics for Short-Form Video
# Patterns extracted from TikTok Shop, Amazon affiliate, and product demo analyses

## How Product Videos Differ from Service Sales

Service sales content (DMs → calls → close) follows a funnel: warm up → qualify → pitch.
Product videos need to convert in ONE viewing. The viewer sees it, wants it, buys it — or scrolls.
There is no nurture sequence. The video IS the entire sales process.

## The Product Video Formula

### 1. Hook: Problem or Curiosity (NOT the product)
Never open with the product. Open with:
- A problem everyone recognizes ("my scalp always does this")
- A bizarre result ("parmesan cheese" over a scalp comb video)
- A bold claim that demands proof ("$5 and it changed my skin")
- Social proof ("this sold out 3 times")

The product appears as the SOLUTION, not the subject.

### 2. Demonstration Over Description
Show, don't tell. The product working IS the content:
- Scalp comb: show the buildup being extracted
- Skincare: show the texture, application, before/after
- Tool: show it solving the problem in real time

Text overlays reinforce what the viewer sees, not replace it.

### 3. Proof Layers (stack multiple)
One claim is ignorable. Multiple proof types build conviction:
- On-screen text citing authority ("Vogue recommended", "dermatologist approved")
- Screenshot of search results or articles
- Before/after comparison
- Comment proof ("everyone asking where I got this")
- Price anchoring ("$5 literally" when it looks expensive)
- Saves signal (high save count = people planning to buy)

### 4. Caption as Sales Copy
TikTok captions function as landing page copy:
- Lead with the benefit, not the product name
- Include price if it's an advantage ("only $5")
- Stack benefit hashtags (#skincare #amazonfinds #glowup)
- "Link in bio" or TikTok Shop tag as CTA

### 5. Hashtag Strategy for Product Videos
Product hashtags signal purchase intent to the algorithm:
- #amazonfinds #tiktokmademebuyit #tiktokshop = shopping audience
- Niche hashtags (#scalpcomb #skincareroutine) = targeted buyers
- Trend hashtags mixed in for reach

## Selling Signals to Detect in Analysis

### In the Video
- TikTok Shop tag/button visible on screen
- Price mentioned in speech or text overlay
- "Link in bio/profile" spoken or written
- Product close-ups, demonstrations, before/after
- Screenshot overlays (search results, articles, reviews)
- Discount/sale/promo code mentioned

### In the Caption
- Price ("$5 literally", "under $20")
- Direct CTA ("link in bio", "shop now", "tap the tag")
- Benefit-first copy
- Product name or brand

### In the Hashtags
- #amazonfinds #tiktokshop #tiktokmademebuyit
- Product-specific tags (#scalpcomb #serumreview)
- #linkinbio #shopnow #musthave

### In the Comments
- "Where did you get this?" / "Link?" = purchase intent
- "My TikTok cart is full" = conversion signal
- "Does it actually work?" = objection (address in video)
- "I just ordered it" = conversion proof
- "Sold out" complaints = scarcity signal
- Price comparisons = shopping behavior

### In the Metrics
- High save count relative to likes = people saving to buy later
- Save-to-like ratio above 0.15 = strong purchase intent signal
- Comments asking for links = direct conversion intent
- Shares to friends = recommendation behavior

## What Makes Product Videos Underperform

1. Opening with the product (looks like an ad, instant scroll)
2. Describing instead of demonstrating
3. No proof (just claims with nothing backing them)
4. Caption that reads like a product listing instead of a story
5. Too many products (pick ONE per video)
6. Revealing the product name before building desire
7. No emotional trigger (problem/curiosity/social proof) before the product appears

## Platform-Specific: TikTok Shop vs Amazon Affiliate vs Link-in-Bio

### TikTok Shop
- Product tag appears on screen — the CTA is built into the platform
- Video can auto-link to the product page
- "Yellow basket" icon signals shopping content
- Viewers can buy without leaving TikTok

### Amazon Affiliate
- "Link in bio" is the CTA (no in-video purchase)
- #amazonfinds signals the source
- Price mention helps ("only $X on Amazon")
- Creator earns commission, doesn't own the product

### Link-in-Bio (own product/Shopify)
- Requires driving traffic off-platform
- Higher friction = needs stronger desire-building
- Often combined with "link in bio" or Linktree
